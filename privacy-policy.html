Talking Clock – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026 · Published by [DEVELOPER NAME]

In short: Talking Clock does not collect, send, sell or share your personal information. Everything you create in the app – alarms, messages, voice recordings and settings – stays on your phone. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and it does not have permission to use the internet.

1. What the app is

Talking Clock is a clock app with alarms, a timer and a stopwatch that can speak out loud – for example, reading an alarm's message or announcing a timer countdown.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • Alarms: the time, repeat days, an optional label, and an optional message to be spoken.
  • Voice messages (optional): if you choose to record your own voice for an alarm, the recording (up to 10 seconds) is saved as an audio file in the app's private storage.
  • Settings and timer schedules: your chosen alarm tone, volumes, vibration, snooze length, and any custom spoken-announcement schedule for the timer.
  • A small technical log: up to about 300 short lines describing events such as "alarm fired" or "speech engine ready", identified only by an internal alarm number. It never contains your alarm labels, messages or recordings.

None of this information is sent to the developer or to anyone else by the app.

3. Information we collect

None. We do not collect personal information, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage statistics or crash reports. The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot transmit data over the internet.

4. Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only so you can record a voice message for an alarm. Recording starts only when you tap the record button, stops automatically after 10 seconds, and the audio is saved only on your phone. The microphone is never used in the background or at any other time.

5. Text-to-speech

When the app speaks, it hands the text to the text-to-speech engine that is built into your phone (for example, Google Text-to-Speech or your manufacturer's engine). That engine is a separate component provided by your device or operating-system vendor, and how it processes text is governed by that provider's own privacy policy. Talking Clock itself does not send the text anywhere.

7. Device backups

If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your Google backup, and restore it on a new phone. That is controlled by your device and Google account settings, not by this app.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Deleting an alarm also deletes its voice recording.
  • You can delete a voice recording at any time from the alarm's edit screen.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes everything the app has stored.

Because we hold no copy of your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Children

Talking Clock is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect any from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy – for example, by adding internet features – this policy will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
